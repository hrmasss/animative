[rising] Then came the hunters.
The MQ-1 Predator. American, built by General Atomics, first flown in 1994. A flying camera… until 2001, when it got missiles.
[shout] Hellfire! The first armed drone strikes, over Afghanistan.
[heavy] Its big brother, the MQ-9 Reaper. A twenty-metre wingspan. More than a day in the air.
And many of its crews? [beat] Sitting in a trailer in Nevada, half a world away.
